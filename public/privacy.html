RegQ Privacy Notice

Version 1.0 · October 2026 · RiskQ, Inc. · Back to RegQ

What we collect

  • Your email address, to sign you in, deliver purchases and record your acceptance of the Terms (with date, Terms version and a one-way hash of your IP address).
  • The company profile you enter (company name, revenue, headcount, industry, locations, data types). It is processed to produce results and is not stored by RegQ after your request, except the company name on paid reports and a short hash of it for advisor report counts.
  • Payment details are collected and stored by Stripe, not by RiskQ.

Service providers

  • Stripe (payments, subscriptions, customer records)
  • Vercel (hosting)
  • Anthropic (Auto-Lookup: the company name you enter is sent to generate suggested profile values)
  • Resend (sign-in emails)

How we use it

To provide RegQ, process payments, prevent abuse, keep records of Terms acceptance, and improve RegQ using aggregated, de-identified usage information. We do not sell personal information.

Retention

Account, payment and Terms-acceptance records are kept as long as needed for the service, legal, tax and dispute purposes.

Your choices

To access, correct or delete your information, email ariel@risk-q.com. Do not enter confidential or personal data beyond what the form asks for.
